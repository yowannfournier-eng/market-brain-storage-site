Privacy Policy

Market Brain Storage is intended for private use by its account owner. With Google OAuth permission, it creates backup files in the account owner's Google Drive and downloads the files it created to verify checksum integrity. It does not request general access to unrelated Drive files. The application uses only the granted Google Drive permissions; OAuth credentials are stored as private GitHub Actions secrets, not on this website. Market data and backup metadata may be included in the created files. Google handles Drive storage under the owner's Google account. Access may be revoked from Google Account settings. GitHub Actions may temporarily retain backup artifacts and logs. This website itself does not collect visitor information through application forms. Contact: use the support address presented on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Personal research backup service. No public trading service.
